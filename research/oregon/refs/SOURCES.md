## 6. How the local analysis was done (so it can be reproduced)
1. Downloaded U2 (official zip) and F2–F6 / L2–L5 to scratch and viewed them.
2. Downloaded C1's `oregon.svg` and stripped the base64 images.
3. Parsed the vector layers with Python `svgelements`, taking each shape's bounding box with transforms applied. Converted root coordinates with `u = root/3.77953 − (617.588, 259.121)`, since svgelements outputs px at the SVG's 3.77953 px/unit.
4. Rendered each floor with `cairosvg` and laid a 10 u grid over it to read room extents and openings.
5. Scale was estimated from the school bus (≈ 77 u ≈ 11.5 m → 0.15 m/u).
