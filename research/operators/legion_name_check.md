## Conclusion
- **No current Siege operator is named "Legion".** No announced one either (through the Y11S4 roadmap as documented).
- "Legion" in Siege refers to the **Keres Legion**, the antagonist faction led by **Deimos**. In the wider Rainbow Six franchise it is also the **"Legion" nanovirus** from *Rainbow Six: Lockdown* (2005).
- **Recommended provisional pick: Lesion** (defender, Gu mines). Researched in `lesion.md`. **Ulo must confirm.**

## Why Lesion is the likely intended pick
1. **Spelling:** "Legion" and "Lesion" differ by one letter (g ↔ s). No other operator name is that close.
2. **Side fits:** the plan lists Legion among the **defenders** (Sentry, Skopós, Mira, Legion, Pulse, Mute). Lesion is a defender. Deimos (the Keres Legion leader) is an attacker and would unbalance the 6/6 roster.
3. **Lore adjacency:** Lesion's current bio is tied to the Keres Legion storyline, so a "Legion" memory could attach to him.
4. **Alternative worth asking about:** the Keres Legion's rank-and-file are "Keres Strikers" and "Keres Sentries", and **Striker and Sentry are already in our roster**. Ulo might have been thinking of the faction itself rather than an operator.
