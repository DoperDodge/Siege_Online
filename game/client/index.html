REDMOND

A private tactical breach shooter on Millrace, our own map. Work in progress.

Phase 6 · in progress

Play a match

Create a match on Millrace or join one: Quick Match, Unranked, Ranked, 1v1, Custom or Practice, two teams of 1–5 (uneven allowed). Lobby, bans, operator pick, prep and action, the defuser, rounds and side swaps, the scoreboard.
Phase 1 · playable

Movement Lab

Walk, sprint, crouch, prone, lean, vault, climb ladders and test stairs, ramps and falls. Pick any of the 12 operators.
Phase 2 · playable

Movement Lab online

Create a room and send a friend the invite link: move, shoot, down and revive each other with the server judging every shot.
Phase 3 · playable

Range Lab

A shooting range with targets out to 50 m that stand, crouch, lie down, lean, strafe and peek: try every weapon and attachment, see the damage against the weapon data, and press F2 to see what the server's hit registration saw.
Phase 3 · playable

Range Lab online

The Range Lab in a room on the server: send a friend the invite link and shoot the targets, or each other, at real latency.
Phase 4 · playable

Destruction Lab

Every kind of wall, door, window, hatch and floor: shoot holes, wallbang, knife, reinforce, barricade, and blow them open with the explosive tools.
Phase 4 · playable

Destruction Lab online

The Destruction Lab in a room on the server: every hole, reinforcement and barricade is the same for everyone in the room.
Phase 8 · in progress

Gadget Lab

Every operator's gadgets on the Destruction Lab's walls: take them out, throw them, place them, set them off.
Phase 8 · in progress

Gadget Lab online

The Gadget Lab in a room on the server: everyone in the room sees the same gadgets.
Project Redmond · playable

Rappel Lab

A yard of walls to rappel on: a three-storey tower, a storey set back behind a ledge, walls a few centimetres apart, a balcony, a roof hanging over its wall, and windows to swing into or climb out of.
Phase 5 · playable

Oregon (greybox)

The map, built from the floor plans: every storey and room, soft walls and floors, hatches, stairs, the ladder, roofs, the outside, the bomb sites and spawns, and rappel. Walk it alone with the lab tools (Go to, the free camera, the plan view) and tell us what's wrong.
Phase 5 · playable

Oregon online

The Oregon greybox in a room on the server: walk it with a friend and break it together.
Project Redmond · first draft

Millrace

Our own map: a closed textile mill by a river, its cellars, offices and boiler house, the open millrace along the west. Four bomb sites and three attacker spawns. Walk it with the lab tools (Go to, the free camera, the plan view) and tell us what's wrong.

Millrace online
