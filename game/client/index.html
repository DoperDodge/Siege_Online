REDMOND

A private fan-made tactical breach shooter on Oregon. Work in progress — Phase 4 of 12.

Phase 1 · playable

Movement Lab

Walk, sprint, crouch, prone, lean, vault, climb ladders and test stairs, ramps and falls. Pick any of the 12 operators.
Phase 2 · playable

Movement Lab online

Create a room and send a friend the invite link: move, shoot, down and revive each other with the server judging every shot.
Phase 3 · playable

Range Lab

A shooting range with targets out to 50 m that stand, crouch, lie down, lean, strafe and peek: try every weapon and attachment, see the damage against the weapon data, and press F2 to see what the server's hit registration saw.
Phase 3 · playable

Range Lab online

The Range Lab in a room on the server: send a friend the invite link and shoot the targets, or each other, at real latency.
Phase 4 · playable

Destruction Lab

Every kind of wall, door, window, hatch and floor: shoot holes, wallbang, knife, reinforce, barricade, and blow them open with the explosive tools.
Phase 4 · playable

Destruction Lab online

The Destruction Lab in a room on the server: every hole, reinforcement and barricade is the same for everyone in the room.
Phase 9

Bot Training

Solo or with friends against bots on Oregon.

Non-commercial fan project. No Ubisoft assets are used.
